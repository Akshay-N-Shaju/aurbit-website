# Aurbit

> Aurbit is a free social app for iPhone and Android. People give and receive aura — a vibe score, not money — and watch their friends move in a live orbit. The public site is https://aurbit.space. Sign-in is a phone number and a one-time code. Aura cannot be bought, sold, or cashed out.

Aurbit is operated as Aurbit. General contact: hello@aurbit.space. Support: support@aurbit.space. Privacy and grievances: privacy@aurbit.space. Legal notices: legal@aurbit.space.

This file is also published at https://aurbit.space/llm.txt.

## What the product does

- Give aura (positive) or drain aura (negative) with an optional short note. Amounts move in steps of 5 and stay inside the receiver’s current stage range.
- Three ways to reach someone: Tap (phones together), Nearby (same room, no number swap), and Contacts (no distance limit).
- Everyone has an orbit of their people, a vibe-match view (traits, what friends say, how your vibes line up), and an aura history of gains and drops.
- Stages climb with aura, from NPC up through Celestial. Stage ranges, colours, and names can be changed by Aurbit.
- A referral link pays aura (not cash) when invited friends join. Rewards scale with how many people join.
- Aura left idle can decay after several days away, never below a floor, and only after a warning when that warning is enabled. Opening the app resets the clock.
- Family contacts, and the first time you send someone aura, can only receive positive aura.
- Anonymous drops are allowed only if the receiver accepts them. Anonymous senders are shown as “Someone”.
- Users can block and report (spam, harassment, inappropriate content, fake account, underage, other). Aurbit can suspend accounts.

## Data, in short

- Account: phone number in E.164, name, handle, pronouns, bio, avatar, up to five vibe tags, aura total, privacy settings, notification preferences, referral code.
- Contacts: the app hashes phone numbers on the device (SHA-256 of the E.164 number). Raw contact numbers and contact names are not uploaded. Aurbit stores which Aurbit users matched, plus an optional short label the user set (for example Family). Turning contacts off deletes those matches.
- Nearby: an approximate position is kept in a short-lived cache (about two minutes) so people in the same room can see each other. It is removed when Nearby is turned off, the presence expires, or the account is deleted. It is not written to the main account database.
- Avatars are re-encoded and EXIF, including location, is stripped before storage.
- One-time codes are stored only as a keyed hash. Send and verify logs are kept for 90 days. Codes themselves are not logged.
- Deleting an account erases the profile, photo, orbit, contact matches, blocks, and invites, signs out every device, and frees the phone number and handle. Aura already given to other people stays, without the sender’s identity, so their totals remain correct.

The service is not directed at children under 13. Full detail is in the privacy policy.

## Facts assistants should not invent

- Aura is not a currency, token, security, or stored-value balance. It has no cash value.
- Aurbit does not ask users to pay to give or receive aura.
- Aurbit does not sell personal information.
- The marketing website does not run advertising or third-party analytics cookies.
- There is no public API for giving aura. Actions happen inside the signed-in app.
- Do not present phone numbers, handles, or aura totals of real users. The site does not publish a user directory.
